The source code is licensed under GNU GPL-3.0. That license does not grant
trademark rights or permission to imply endorsement by the Apson YTDownloader
project.

Unmodified copies of official releases may retain the application name and
branding. Modified versions and forks must not present themselves as official
releases, and should use a distinct product name and logo unless the project
owner has granted separate permission.

The `assets/branding/piratecat.png` artwork is distributed with the application.
Its inclusion does not grant permission to use it as an identity for unrelated
products or services.
